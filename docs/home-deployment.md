# Home deployment notes

These notes describe the current household deployment layered on top of the generic candlelight package.

## Daily schedule

- Indoor non-bathroom lights fade up with sunrise and reach the morning target.
- The morning taper finishes at 10:00 AM.
- Clear daytime conditions leave indoor lights off.
- Overcast, rain, snow, hail, or fog may hold already eligible indoor lights at 15%.
- Sunset starts the evening fade; natural candlelight remains the evening default from 7:30 PM.
- Bathroom lights are excluded from the daily controller and remain independently controlled.
- Manual off holds take priority and the daily helper resets at 4:00 AM.

## Current light mapping

- Kitchen: Kitchen Counter, Kitchen Fridge, Kitchen Sink, Kitchen Dishwasher
- Dining room: Dining Room 1, Dining Room 2
- Living room: Living Room 1–4

Kitchen Dishwasher was restored to service and removed from the broken-light exclusions.

## Holiday scene plan

The kitchen, dining-room, and living-room lights are the holiday zone. Planned sunset-to-11:00 PM scenes are:

- Halloween: orange and purple
- Thanksgiving: warm amber and soft gold
- Christmas: red, green, and warm white
- New Year's Eve: blue, violet, and white
- Valentine's Day: red and pink
- July 4th: red, white, and blue

Holiday scenes should apply only during their holiday windows, yield to manual off holds, and leave bathroom lighting independent.
